# Vision module: supervised localization — 2026-11-10

## Why and how

A CNN observes only a `3×64×64` RGB frame in DeepMind Lab's 2.5m × 2.5m square arena. Ground-truth position and heading are used **as supervision labels**, not image-model input. The CNN has Conv2d 3→16→32→64→128 and a 256-d embedding, followed by 256 place-cell and 12 head-direction-cell output logits.

**Data collection:** approximately 1,000 episodes (~1,300 images/episode, ~1.3M total collected images). Do not confuse collected images with raw DMLab frame steps. **Optimization:** **100,000 supervised optimizer updates**, minibatch size 32 (3,200,000 image presentations, potentially duplicates). Validation N=10,000 held-out samples. Training source: `collect_vision_rh_1000_v2.py`, `train_vision_combined.py`, `grid_cells/vision.py`.

Local-only trained model: `data/checkpoints/vision_rh_combined_1000/latest.pt`. Check its `update` field for exact step and `dataset_fingerprint` for data directories. Do not claim model weights are uploaded to GitHub.

## Results

- Mean position error: **26.3cm**; median **19.8cm**; approximately **73.9% ≤30cm**.
- Mean heading error **19.2°**.
- Scatter visualizes positions contracted toward the center compared with ground truth.
- Inner arena mean error **~12.3cm** vs near-wall **~43.7cm** (true position near-wall defined as `abs(x)>1m OR abs(y)>1m`).
- C heatmap bins true positions into **16×16** squares (2.5m total width), with per-bin sample coverage.

## Key methodological caveat

The reported \\(x,y\\) prediction is **not directly regressed by CNN**. It is the PC-probability weighted mean:

`pprob = softmax(place_logits, dim=-1)` then `pred_xy = pprob @ pc.means`.

Such averaging over PC centers *within the arena* can compress the estimated coordinate toward the center and increase edge/corner error even with perfect probability prediction. A separate experiment is needed to attribute additional error to camera visibility. **Distal cues being obscured at walls remains a hypothesis**. Proposed test: same true positions, different heading orientation facing the wall versus inward, holding other conditions fixed, measuring images and decoding errors. Inspect training-set spatial sampling too.

**RL caveat**: current Oracle Agent takes ground-truth current PC/HD, NOT this CNN's decoded 26.3cm-error position.
